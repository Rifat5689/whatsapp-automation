# RIFAT.AI Project

RIFAT.AI is a single-owner AI-assisted WhatsApp communication system.

## Goals
- Receive WhatsApp messages.
- Identify contacts and relationships.
- Apply contact-specific rules and owner personalization.
- Generate context-aware replies in Bangla, Banglish, and English.
- Support AI Auto, AI Approval, and Manual modes.
- Allow human takeover and a global emergency stop.
- Manage and safely share approved personal photos.
- Track AI actions, approvals, sends, and delivery states.

## Primary Pages
- Conversations
- Friends & Rules
- Personalization
- AI Models
- Activity & Tracking
- Settings

`My Photos` belongs inside `Personalization → My Photos` and is not a primary sidebar page.

## Scope
Initial product is for one owner. Do not add multi-tenant SaaS complexity unless requirements change.
