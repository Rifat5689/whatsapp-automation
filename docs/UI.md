# UI

## Primary Navigation
- Conversations
- Friends & Rules
- Personalization
- AI Models
- Activity & Tracking
- Settings

## Conversations
Show contact list, active chat, contact/rule summary, text/images, AI/manual indicators, approval drafts, delivery states, retry controls, takeover/resume controls.

Conversation modes:
- AI Auto
- AI Approval
- Manual

## Friends & Rules
Manage contacts, relationships, language, communication style, AI state, conversation mode, notes, memory permissions, photo permissions, and approval rules.

## Personalization
Tabs:
- General
- My Photos

My Photos supports upload, preview, rename, description, category, primary photo, sharing settings, auto-send, filters, replace, and delete.

## AI Models
Manage provider, model, primary/fallback model, and provider status.

## Activity & Tracking
Show important AI, approval, messaging, photo, and system events.

## Settings
Global AI toggle, emergency stop, privacy defaults, integration status, and account/application settings.

Keep the UI simple, responsive, and operational rather than decorative.
